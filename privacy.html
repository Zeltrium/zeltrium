4. Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

We count visits with GoatCounter to see which pages and links are used and where visitors come from. GoatCounter is run by Martin Tournoij, Ireland, and stores its data on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany and Finland.

What is sent. When a page loads, a small script hosted on this website sends GoatCounter the page address, the page title, the referring page and, if you followed one of our links, its ref label. When you click a download, buy or brief button, it also sends the name of that button, and after you submit a free-pack form, which form it was. Your email address is never sent to GoatCounter.

What is not stored. Like any web request, the request carries your IP address and your browser's user agent. GoatCounter uses them only to work out the browser, operating system, language and country, and to avoid counting the same visit twice within eight hours. It does not store your IP address or the full user agent, and it keeps only totals per day or hour. No cookies are set and nothing is read from or stored in your browser. We only see aggregate numbers, such as "40 visits from Reddit today", and cannot identify you from them.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how this website is used so we can improve it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time (section 9), or block the requests to goatcounter.com with any content blocker. See the GoatCounter privacy policy.

Double opt-in. After you submit the form, you receive an email asking you to confirm your address. We only add you to the mailing list and send further emails after you confirm. If you don't confirm, MailerLite keeps your address as unconfirmed and we send you nothing else. We log the time of your sign-up and confirmation and the IP address used, so that we can prove your consent.

Where you came from. Together with your email address, the form sends one short label saying how you found us: the ref tag of the link you followed (for example reddit) or the domain of the site that linked here (for example dev.to). We use it only to see which channels bring people, as part of the same consent. Nothing is stored in your browser for this.

Provider. We use MailerLite to collect addresses and send emails. MailerLite Limited, 88 Harcourt Street, Dublin 2, D02 DK18, Ireland, processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. According to MailerLite, data is stored in the European Union. What we track in emails. We don't track whether you open our emails: they contain no tracking pixel. Links in our emails pass through MailerLite's servers, which record that a link was clicked, so we can see which content is useful. This is part of the same consent and ends when you unsubscribe.
