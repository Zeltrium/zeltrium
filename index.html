Questions
Before you ask.

    

      
What formats do I get?
Every animation comes as a Lottie .json file and as .lottie, the compressed format, in two color sets: dark (light lines for dark UIs) and light (dark lines for light UIs). Same animation, pick what your player and your background prefer. Files are 1–18 KB, durations 0.27–0.87 s.

      
Will they work on light and dark backgrounds?
Yes. Both sets ship in the pack, and the accent stays the same in both. If your app switches themes, load the matching file.

      
Will they match my icons?
They are drawn on the Lucide grid with a 2 px stroke at 24 px, so they sit next to Lucide, Heroicons or Tabler without looking borrowed. Built for 16–48 px.

      
Where do they work?
Anywhere Lottie plays: the web (lottie-web, dotLottie), React and React Native, iOS, Android, Flutter, Framer and Webflow.

      
Can I change the colors?
Yes. Every color is flat and has one of four roles: line, accent, progress track and off control. Swap one value per role in a Lottie editor, in After Effects, or in code.

      
Can I use them in client and commercial work?
Yes. Use them in unlimited personal, commercial and client projects, no attribution needed. You can't resell or redistribute the files themselves. Read the license.

      
What if it's not for me?
Email zeltriumstudio@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase and you get a full refund. No questions asked.

      
Are more packs coming?
Yes. UI States is next: loading, success, error, empty and offline screens. Get the free 5 and you'll hear about it first.
