Process

How a project runs.

  1. BriefYou send the brief. I reply with questions, a timeline and a quote.
  2. StoryboardKey frames before any animation, so the idea is agreed while changes are cheap.
  3. AnimationA draft first, then revisions. Two rounds are included.
  4. HandoffFiles ready to build, checked in a browser.

Async by default: written updates, no calls needed.

Questions

Before you ask.

Can you work under our brand?

Yes, that's the default. Your client sees your studio, not me.

Will you contact our client?

Never, unless you ask me to.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, happy to sign yours.

What files do we get?

After Effects source files and whatever the project needs: video in MP4 or ProRes in the formats you ask for, or Lottie JSON and .lottie with named markers and notes for your developers.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds. The storyboard is approved before animation starts, so big changes happen early, when they're cheap.

How long does it take?

It depends on scope. You get a timeline with the quote.

Can we start small?

Yes. A single animation is a good first project.

Do we need calls?

No. I work async with written updates, so nothing waits for a free slot in anyone's calendar.

Where are you based?

Germany, CET. I reply within 24 hours. Invoices in EUR or USD.
